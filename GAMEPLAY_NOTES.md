# Gameplay systems

- **Run:** A new run rolls one ball type. The type remains fixed until the run ends. Three balls are available by default. Launching spends one ball; unused balls and current HP carry to the second boss. The two bosses have 200 and 1,000 HP. HP reaching zero or having no ball left after a turn ends the run. Defeating the second boss wins.
- **Save:** `Continue` starts a fresh run with the saved currency and permanent upgrades. `New Game` clears both for a showcase reset. Leaving an active battle ends that run, but earned currency remains. Backing out of the roll screen preserves the pending ball and spent rerolls. The profile is stored in `user://pin_roulette_profile.json`.
- **Currency:** 20% of actual damage dealt, rounded down across the run. Gold multiplies both score and the resulting currency by five; it does not multiply HP, speed, or healing. Overkill awards only the enemy's remaining HP as actual damage.
- **Upgrades:** A starting ball adds one; rerolls add one pre-run reroll; flat damage adds 5 per scoring turn; HP adds 20 maximum HP; money adds 25% to currency reward per level. Current starting costs are 20, 15, 10, 10, and 20 respectively, increasing linearly by level. These values are in `scripts/core/RunProfile.gd` and the gameplay menu script.
- **Element chart:** The checked-in CSV is the source. Rows are bumper elements and columns are ball elements. The multiplier applies to that hit's points. Ice and Wind bumper speed changes stack by 0.2 each until drain. Steel bumper cancels charged bonus damage on the next enemy attack. Fairy heals 20% max HP, or 40% for a Fairy ball. Dark adds 20% dodge chance per hit for the next attack. Ghost guarantees a dodge; Psychic reflects 20% of the next enemy attack to the enemy. Three main bumpers get random elements at each run start. Five total drop-target hits trigger a random element, including Psychic and Ghost.
- **Tunable choices:** Enemy base attack remains 15. Charges arrive every 15 seconds while a ball is live and add 5 damage each. P within 0.35 seconds of a charge parries that charge. Electric strengthens reaction multipliers and effects by 1.5; Gold uses the Normal chart column because the CSV has no Gold column. These choices can be adjusted in `scripts/core/TurnManager.gd` and the Electric ball resource.
- **Ball art:** Each resource in `resources/ball_types/` has an empty `ball_texture` slot. Assign the matching texture there when art is ready. Until then, the existing Bevo ball texture is retained; no new placeholder ball art was added.
